Why Romania

A near-shore CNC partner inside the EU

  • EU member state: goods move freely within the single market – no customs, no duties, intra-community B2B supply with VAT reverse charge.
  • Everything in-house in one workshop: requirements, design, simulation, CNC turning and milling, assembly, testing and delivery.
  • Prototypes, one-off parts, spare parts and small to medium series for the automotive, industrial, agricultural and energy sectors.
  • Quotes in EUR; we work in English and Romanian.
  • Road freight to most of Central and Western Europe in a few working days.
